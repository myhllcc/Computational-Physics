# Two-dimensional Ising Model

Yunhao Mo · Imperial College London · 2023

MATLAB Metropolis simulation of spin dynamics, energy, magnetisation and specific heat, comparing local energy updates with whole-lattice recalculation.

- [Code](src/), [saved data](data/), [report](reports/report.pdf)

In MATLAB, open `src/` and select `single_T_big_N.m`, `Multi_T.m` or `CompareTwoVersion.m`. Results and timings have not been rerun.
