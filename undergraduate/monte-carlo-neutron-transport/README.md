# Monte Carlo Neutron Transport

Yunhao Mo · University of Manchester · 2021

Simulates neutron transport in water, lead and graphite using inverse-transform sampling and three-dimensional random walks. Repeated simulations and weighted regression estimate uncertainty and attenuation lengths.

- [Notebook](notebooks/original_project3.ipynb)
- [Report](reports/report.pdf)

Open in Jupyter with NumPy and Matplotlib. The course `randssp` comparison helper is included in the notebook. Saved results have not been rerun.
